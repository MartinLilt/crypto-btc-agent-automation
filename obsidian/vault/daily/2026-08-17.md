## Order book / whales / detection — discussion (nothing built)

Thought about trading order flow / following whales / bumps & squeezes.

- **Book depth + tick bumps → HFT-only mirage** (seconds-scale, spoofed, co-located
  wins). Not our game.
- **"Follow the whales via the book" paradox:** because whales don't want to lose,
  they make themselves INVISIBLE (iceberg / TWAP / OTC / spoof). Visible walls are
  usually bait aimed at retail. And smart money DOES lose (gets squeezed).
- **Detecting the hiding** (icebergs/spoofs) is technically possible at second-scale
  but a poor bet: needs an always-on L2 engine, Binance depth is coarse (no order
  IDs), signal local/ambiguous, adversarial arms race; OTC invisible.
- **Accessible edge = money whales CAN'T hide:** exchange netflows (on-chain),
  funding/OI crowding, CVD — slower, un-spoofable, EU-legal to read.
- **Squeeze angle:** funding/OI extremes build over hours → only hourly polling
  needed (not HFT); but EU-spot plays only the long side (short-squeeze pumps),
  and OI/liq history is ~30d (funding is long) → backtest partly data-constrained.

**Recommended cheap next step (not taken):** backtest exchange-netflows or
funding/OI-extreme → reversal on the basket before building anything.
